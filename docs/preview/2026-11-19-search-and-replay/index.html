Search and replay

Two read-only views over what already happened between you, Firstmate and its crew. Nothing new is started, and nothing goes around Firstmate. To do something again, you ask Firstmate in the conversation, as today.

How DeepSeek does it. DeepSeek's harness adds a "Trajectory" tab to each conversation. It is a list of turns (each message and what followed it), with every model step and tool call underneath. It has a search box and fold controls. Clicking a step opens a panel with Summary, Input, Result and Timing. There is also a timing strip across the top. Their "replay" means walking back through what happened, not running it again. I kept their list, search and step panel, worded them plainly, and left out the timing strip and token counts to keep the screen calm.

1. Search

Where: the existing Search (⌘K or the magnifier). Nothing new on any screen.

Workflow

  1. Press ⌘K and type a few words.
  2. Results come in groups: In conversations (what you wrote and what Firstmate answered), In what Firstmate did (each step it took, such as handing work to a secondmate or checking on crew), then documents, work and places as today.
  3. Click a result. The conversation opens with "What happened" beside it, at that step.

Today the search box already has a place for words inside conversations, but nothing answers it. The search code exists in Amun's work store and only needs connecting.

2. Replay: "What happened"

Where: the pane beside a conversation that already opens documents, the PRD, the backlog and the browser.

With the pane open, the conversation header is too narrow to show its links (PRD, Backlog, and so on). That is how the app works today, so the "What happened" link sits with them and is hidden in this picture.

Workflow

  1. Open "What happened" from a conversation, or by clicking a search result.
  2. Each block is one turn: a message you sent, or something that woke Firstmate (a secondmate reporting back, a scheduled check).
  3. Under it, in order, every step Firstmate took, in plain words, with the Firstmate script it ran (for example fm-spawn) and how long it took.
  4. Click a step to see Summary, What it sent, What came back and Timing.
  5. Previous step and Next step walk through it one step at a time. Search this conversation narrows the steps. Fold all shows only the turns.

Where the information comes from

  • Your messages and Firstmate's replies: Amun's own conversation store, as today.
  • Firstmate's steps: the record Paseo already keeps of every First Mate turn. Amun only reads it.
  • Not in this first version: the inside of each secondmate's own work. That lives in Firstmate's crew sessions (you already see it with "Watch"). Firstmate's activity ledger could add it later.
