Your question: what do you see each time Amun opens?

  • First the ram with “Loading Amun”, then Home, every time. It does not reopen the chat you were last in.
  • Home shows the date, Needs your attention, Your work as tiles, three suggestions and the message box.
  • A message typed there goes to Firstmate in General, or creates new work when you ask for it.
  • The Home tile in the sidebar brings you back to this screen.

This round changes nothing on Home. Whether Amun should open on your last chat is still your open question from 19 November.

1. The sidebar, laid out like ChatGPT

Workflow it enables: see every folder and its chats at a glance, and start a new chat in a folder with one click.

  • Your work lists one folder per product, project, task and RFP, with a folder picture that opens when the folder is open, as in your screenshot.
  • Pointing at a folder shows two buttons on its row: the three dots (Open, Rename, Delete, as today) and New chat, the pencil-on-paper button from your screenshot.
  • Chats sit under their folder, each named from your first message. “Main chat” is gone; your existing conversations stay as the first chat in each folder.
  • Chats lists the chats that belong to no folder, newest first. This is what “General” was; it has its own New chat button.
  • The eight tiles, Firstmate’s card and the bottom row stay where they are. Nothing is added to the message box.

Two things I assumed: the heading says “Your work” (the name Home already uses) where ChatGPT says “Projects”, because the folders are also products, tasks and RFPs; and both headings fold their list when clicked, as ChatGPT’s do.

Starting a new chat in a folder

  • An empty chat opens in that folder, with the usual message box.
  • It is listed as “New chat” until your first message names it.
  • /new typed in any chat does the same in that chat’s folder.
  • Firstmate is told it is a fresh topic in Gitex: it gets Gitex’s own facts and the names of its other chats, and nothing said in them.

The menu on a chat

  • Rename changes the chat’s name in place.
  • Move to another folder lets you pick the folder yourself, for when Amun did not suggest it.
  • Archive takes it out of the list. It is still found by Search and can be brought back.
  • There is no Delete on a chat: chats are kept.

2. A request for new work typed inside another folder

Workflow it enables: you are in a Gitex chat and ask for a new product. Amun holds the message and asks where it goes, before Firstmate sees it.

  • As you suggested, the question shows in Firstmate’s card on the left, where its questions already appear, not in the conversation. I agree: it is one place to look, it is visible from every screen, and it reuses what is already built for questions.
  • The conversation only shows your message, marked “Not sent yet”.
  • The two choices are real buttons. Move to a new folder creates the folder (you can change its name first), files your message as its first chat, opens it and sends it to Firstmate there.
  • Keep it in Gitex sends the message in this chat, and Amun does not ask about that name in this folder again.
  • The same question is listed under Needs your attention on Home, like every other question.
  • If you type something else first, that message is sent as usual. The held one waits, also after you close and reopen Amun.

One cost to know about: Firstmate’s card keeps its space, so while it asks, the folder list above it has less room and scrolls, as it does today for any question. You can fold the card to one line with its arrow.

Workflow it enables: see where a conversation went, and get there in one click.

  • The new folder appears at the top of Your work, as the most recently used.
  • The Gitex chat keeps one quiet line, Continued in AMUN-SDK, with Open.
  • Work Firstmate already handed to the crew is not stopped by a move. It stops only if you ask Firstmate.

4. When a chat drifts

Workflow it enables: a chat wanders to another subject after a few turns, and ends up in the right folder with one click.

  • Firstmate says in its reply that the subject has changed. Its card on the left then asks; there is no second model.
  • Move them to a new folder re-files those messages as a chat in the new folder.
  • Keep them in Gitex changes nothing and is remembered.
  • When Firstmate is not sure, it says nothing and the chat stays where it is.

5. A name you already have

Workflow it enables: never end up with two folders for the same thing (“Gitex” and “gitex”, “Amun SDK” and “AMUN-SDK”).

  • Names are compared without regard to capitals, spaces and dashes, archived folders included.
  • An archived folder offers Bring it back in place of Open.

What happens without a card

  • “Create a new project for Gitex to be high priority and ready on Dec 6”, typed on Home, in a chat that has no folder, or on Portfolio: the Gitex folder is created straight away with the priority and 6 December 2026, your message becomes its first chat, and Firstmate starts. No card.
  • “Create a landing page for Gitex” is not new work, so it stays where it was typed.
  • If anything goes wrong while deciding, the message goes where you typed it. A message is never lost.

What does not change

  • One Firstmate with one memory. A chat is never its own agent.
  • Home, the eight tiles, Firstmate’s card, the message box and the conversation header stay as they are.
  • Forks keep working as approved on 19 November 2026. A fork is listed under its folder like any other chat.
