Two things this needs from you Your picture has five columns: New, In progress, On hold, Won, Lost. Amun has seven stages today: Received, In response, Submitted, Won, Handed to delivery, Lost, Withdrawn. I propose: New = Received; In progress = In response and Submitted; Won includes Handed to delivery; Lost includes Withdrawn; On hold is a new status with a reason.

The tiles and cards also show things Amun does not store yet: estimated value, the next step and its date, dated summaries, and the people on the bid. These would be new fields on an RFP.
