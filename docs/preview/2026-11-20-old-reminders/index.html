1. A tick box on a project’s task

Workflow it enables: say a task under a project is complete with one click, as you asked.

  • A reminder Firstmate filed under a project (its name starts with the project, as in “MoCA: …”) shows as Task · MoCA with a tick box.
  • A reminder that belongs to no project shows as Reminder, without a box.
  • Ticking asks Firstmate to close the task and record that you completed it (Firstmate’s own fm-captain-hold.sh answer --release).
  • The line says “Done. Firstmate is closing it.” and offers Undo for a few seconds, before anything is sent.
  • Once Firstmate has closed it, it moves under Done.

2. The “…” menu

Workflow it enables: put a reminder off properly, close one that is no longer needed, or get help doing it.

  • Done, close it does the same as the tick box. It is the way to close a reminder that has no box.
  • Remind me later asks Firstmate to put it off to that day with its own date (hold --until). It stays away after restarts and comes back that day. Today’s Snooze sends nothing to Firstmate and fails without saying so.
  • Help me do it is for a reminder you have to act on, such as the two emails.

3. Help me do it

Workflow it enables: as you asked, a reminder to write something comes with a draft, or questions when Firstmate lacks the context.

  • Amun sends your request to Firstmate in your latest chat.
  • Firstmate gives it to the crewmate whose job it is (the Writer for an email). When it has too little to go on, it interviews you one question at a time first.
  • The draft comes back in the chat for you to edit. Nothing is sent to anyone.
  • The reminder stays open until you tick it or choose Done.
